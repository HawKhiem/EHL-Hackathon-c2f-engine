
Before calling an ancillary line covered, read the policy's "outlay that is not a repair cost" / "only the first such charge" style clauses and apply them ACROSS the invoices and trades of the same claim, not only within one invoice: (a) a contractor's own measuring, testing or diagnostic-device line (moisture measurement, thermal imaging, leak-sensor readings and the like) that is separate from the visit which located the source of the damage is that contractor's own equipment and is not covered where the policy excludes the contractor's own diagnostic and measuring devices; (b) where the policy indemnifies the same item of cost only once per insured event across contractors, a second "construction waste disposal", "vehicle/travel", "call-out" or "administration" line on a later invoice is a duplicate and not covered - put its would-be price in t_if_covered. Do not apply either rule when the policy has no such clause.
